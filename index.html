My Story

The journey that changed how I work and live.

This is a personal experience, written from my perspective. I am sharing what I went through and what helped me—not presenting it as a treatment plan for anyone else.

2014 — Career begins

I started my IT career in Delhi. I was highly focused on learning, work and building my future. Long hours at a computer became normal.

September–October 2016

The first signs were tightness around my neck, shoulders and upper back. Then vertigo appeared. What I thought might be weakness became the beginning of a frightening period.

2016–2017

The worst period

This was the hardest stage of my journey. I had severe pain, vertigo, vomiting, difficulty standing and walking, and difficulty sleeping. I became afraid of going far from home because I did not know when vertigo might happen.

Severe symptoms

Pain, vertigo, vomiting and difficulty with basic daily activities.

Treatment

I consulted doctors, including doctors in Karnal, Haryana, and went through medicines and physiotherapy.

Life became smaller

Travel was limited. My priority became getting back to a normal life rather than pushing myself to do everything.

My Recovery Timeline

2016 → 2020 → later years → today

2016

The problem starts

Neck/upper-back tightness was followed by vertigo and then severe symptoms. Physiotherapy and medical treatment began.

2016–2017

The hardest period

Medicine, physiotherapy and a lot of rest. I struggled with walking, standing, sleeping and travelling far from home.

2020

Physiotherapy again

My treatment was not a perfectly straight line. I stopped and restarted physiotherapy at different points. I also took medicines prescribed to me, including Vertin at different doses.

LATER YEARS

Focus on myself

I stepped away from work for a period and focused on exercise, stretching, walking, rest and posture. I reduced unnecessary laptop, phone and TV use and walked twice a day.

A NEW ROUTINE

Getting back into life

I started cricket umpiring in local matches, using my experience and getting outside again. I also began looking for another job.

2024–2026

Back to professional life

I returned to development work. Today I work in an MNC, go to the office, drive, travel and continue exercising while maintaining a more careful work setup.

Before I understood the problem

My Mistakes

I don't blame my younger self. I simply did not know better.

No regular exercise

Exercise was not part of my routine when I started my IT career.

Taking stress too seriously

I carried work and other stress with me instead of learning to step away and recover.

Poor posture

I leaned forward toward the laptop and could stay in that position for hours.

Long driving and riding

I did not pay enough attention to position and breaks during longer car or bike journeys.

Poor sleeping posture

I did not think much about how my sleeping position affected comfort and support.

Too much screen time

Long laptop and phone sessions became normal, often without enough breaks.

What Helped Me

Small changes became my new habits.

My recovery was not one magic fix. It was a combination of treatment, physiotherapy, stretching, walking, rest, posture awareness and lifestyle changes.

What I Do Today

I did not leave technology. I learned to work differently.

I am still a developer. I still use computers every day. The difference is that health is now part of my work routine.

Better workstation

I use a larger screen, ergonomic keyboard and ergonomic mouse, and pay attention to comfortable screen distance and posture.

Exercise twice a day

Exercise is now a regular part of my daily routine rather than something I do only when I have a problem.

Breaks and movement

I use breaks, including my lunch break, to get away from the screen and move around.

Better stress management

I try not to carry every work problem with me. Work matters, but it should not consume the whole day.

Office and travel

I can go to the office by car, travel, work in an MNC and continue my professional responsibilities.

Life outside work

I protect time for exercise, rest and life away from the computer. Weekends are ideally kept away from unnecessary work.

What I Learned

My rules today

01 Exercise is part of my day.
02 I don't ignore persistent discomfort.
03 I consciously maintain a comfortable posture.
04 I avoid sitting continuously for hours.
05 I keep screens at a comfortable working position.
06 I take breaks from prolonged screen use.
07 I rest when my body needs rest.
08 I try to protect time away from work.
“Work hard for your career, but don't forget the body that is helping you build it.”
